MARKETING GLOSSARY

Marketing words, explained like a human.

No wall of jargon. Find the meaning, understand why it matters and get back to work.

A TO Z

Definitions worth keeping close.

SEO

Search engine optimisation. Improving a website so useful pages can be found through organic search.

PPC

Pay per click. An advertising model in which a click is a common billing action.

CPC

Cost per click. Spend divided by the number of clicks measured.

CPM

Cost per thousand impressions. A way to compare the cost of advertising exposure.

CTR

Click-through rate. Clicks divided by impressions, shown as a percentage.

CPA

Cost per acquisition or action. Spend divided by tracked conversions.

CPL

Cost per lead. Advertising spend divided by leads recorded.

ROAS

Return on ad spend. Tracked revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend.

ROI

Return on investment. A return measure that needs a clearly defined benefit and cost.

Conversion

A user action that matters to the business, such as a purchase or completed enquiry.

GA4

Google Analytics 4. A tool for understanding website and app events.

GTM

Google Tag Manager. A system used to manage approved website tags and tracking.

Search Console

A Google tool showing how a website appears and performs in Google Search.

Landing page

The page someone visits after clicking an ad or link.

Retargeting

Advertising to people who previously interacted with your business where permitted.

UTM

URL parameters used to help label traffic sources and campaigns.

Search intent

The reason behind a search, such as learning, comparing or buying.

Keyword

A word or phrase used to describe the topic of a search or a page.

Negative keyword

A Google Ads exclusion used to avoid showing ads for unwanted searches.

Performance Max

A Google Ads campaign type that uses assets and automation across eligible Google inventory.

Organic traffic

Visits from unpaid search results or other non-paid sources, depending on the reporting definition.

Local SEO

Search optimisation focused on customers in a real geographic service area.

LCP

Largest Contentful Paint. A web performance metric for main content loading.

INP

Interaction to Next Paint. A responsiveness metric for user interactions.

CLS

Cumulative Layout Shift. A metric for unexpected visual page movement.

A/B test

A structured comparison between two versions of something, ideally with a clear hypothesis.
