PRIVACY POLICY

Your information should be handled with care.

This page explains how the ARAT website handles enquiries and what happens when you choose to contact us through third-party services.

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Who we are

ARAT is a digital marketing agency based in Madhyapur Thimi, Bhaktapur, Nepal. You can contact us by calling +977 9844430480 or by using the contact options on this website.

Information you choose to share

The contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number, website or business name, service interests, optional budget and your message. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive information through the form.

How the form works

This website is designed as a static site. It does not automatically store or transmit your form data to an ARAT server. When you choose “Open enquiry in WhatsApp”, your browser prepares a WhatsApp message containing the information you entered. You must press Send in WhatsApp to deliver the message. WhatsApp may process your data under its own privacy policy. The “Copy my enquiry” option copies the text locally to your clipboard when browser permissions allow it.

How we use enquiries

When you send a message to ARAT, we use the information to understand your request, respond, discuss services and manage follow-up. We do not add you to a mailing list by submitting this form.

Third-party links and embedded content

Our website links to WhatsApp, Facebook and Google Maps. The contact page also includes a Google Maps embed. These services may process usage information under their own terms and privacy notices. External fonts are requested from Google Fonts where available.

Analytics and cookies

The supplied earlier website included analytics-related scripts. This rebuild does not intentionally include third-party marketing pixels or campaign tracking tags. If such tools are added later, the appropriate privacy and consent notices must be reviewed before publishing.

Data retention and your choices

We keep enquiry conversations only for as long as necessary for the relevant business purpose and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to review, correct or delete information you sent by contacting us through the methods on the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy when our tools or processes change. The current version will appear on this page.
