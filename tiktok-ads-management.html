FAQ

TikTok Ads Management: common questions.

Does TikTok Ads work for Nepal businesses?

The answer depends on your audience, offer and account eligibility. We assess the fit before recommending spend.

Can you use our existing videos?

Yes. We review existing footage and suggest edits, hooks and formats that better fit the platform.

What is a good TikTok Ads result?

It depends on your objective. Reach may be useful for awareness, while leads or website actions matter more for direct response.

Do you only work with creators?

No. We can plan tests across user-generated, brand-made and graphic-led creatives.

What if we do not have professional TikTok videos?

We can start with simple phone footage, clear scripts and a good hook. Production value helps, but the idea and the audience matter more.
