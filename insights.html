Insights

How I think about paid-media performance.

Practical principles from campaign management, optimisation and measurement.

Google Ads

Search intent matters more than raw click volume.

Strong search performance starts with understanding why the query exists, then controlling match types, search terms, negatives, landing-page relevance and conversion quality.

Meta Ads

Cheap reach is not the same as useful demand.

Reach and engagement are valuable only when we understand what happens next — profile visits, site behaviour, reservations, leads or sales.

Creative Strategy

The hook earns attention; the offer earns action.

Creative testing should separate the first-second hook, product proof, emotional reason, offer and CTA so we know what is actually moving performance.

Tracking

Optimisation is only as good as the signal.

GTM, GA4, pixels and UTMs create the measurement layer that lets us distinguish traffic volume from meaningful behaviour.

Reporting

A report should explain what changed and why.

Good reporting finds anomalies, connects upper-funnel movement with conversion behaviour and turns data into next actions.

Scaling

Scale winners without scaling waste.

Budget growth should follow evidence: audience quality, conversion stability, creative durability and the economics of the business.
