About me

Performance marketing with equal focus on strategy, psychology and numbers.

I work across the full advertising journey — understanding what makes an audience act, building the right campaign structure, tracking behaviour and turning performance data into the next decision.

I don’t see paid media as “launch an ad and wait.”

My approach connects ad copy, customer psychology, creative direction, targeting, retargeting, analytics, tracking and optimisation. That means looking beyond surface metrics and asking whether the campaign is creating the business outcome it was built for.
