FAQ

Analytics, Tracking & Reporting: common questions.

Can you set up Google Analytics 4 for my website?

Yes. We can review the site, plan events and implement GA4 through suitable tools.

What is Google Tag Manager used for?

It helps manage approved analytics and conversion tags without editing website code for every small change.

Does analytics guarantee accurate attribution?

No system gives perfect attribution. We explain limits and use more than one source where useful.

Will you explain the reports in simple terms?

Yes. The report should show what changed, what it might mean and what to do next.

What if our analytics setup is broken or incomplete?

That is a good place to start. We review your current tags, events and reporting. We document what is missing before proposing changes.
