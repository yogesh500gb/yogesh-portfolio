FAQ

Facebook & Instagram Ads: common questions.

Are Facebook and Instagram Ads good for small businesses?

They can work well when the offer, budget, creative and follow-up process are clear. We start with the problem, not a fixed package.

Can you manage both Facebook and Instagram campaigns?

Yes. Both platforms can be managed through Meta Ads Manager with a joined-up plan.

Do we need new videos every week?

Not always. We plan a practical creative test schedule based on available content and campaign performance.

Can we track enquiries from Meta Ads?

We can configure appropriate events and review platform reporting alongside what your business actually receives.

Do we need a large budget for Facebook and Instagram ads?

Not necessarily. We can start with one audience and a few strong creatives. We agree a test budget first. Results depend on the offer, market and landing page.
