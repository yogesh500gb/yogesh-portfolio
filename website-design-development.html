FAQ

Website Design & Development: common questions.

Can you build a website for a small business in Nepal?

Yes. We can scope a practical website around your main services and budget.

Will the website be SEO-ready?

We include a clean structure, meaningful headings, metadata and internal links as part of the build.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can audit the current experience and prioritise changes rather than rebuilding without a reason.

Does the price include hosting and domain?

Hosting and domain costs depend on the chosen provider and are agreed before work starts.

Do we need a completely new website to get better enquiries?

Not always. Sometimes a clearer headline, faster page or simpler form makes the biggest difference. We review what can be improved first.
