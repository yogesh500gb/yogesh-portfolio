FAQ

Digital marketing in Nepal: useful answers.

Is ARAT a digital marketing agency in Kathmandu?

ARAT is based in Madhyapur Thimi, Bhaktapur, within Kathmandu Valley. We serve businesses in Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur and across Nepal.

Can ARAT support affordable digital marketing for small businesses?

Yes. We can start with a focused service or audit and grow the scope when it is useful. Fees depend on agreed deliverables.

Which digital marketing services are most useful for a new business?

Start with your audience and offer. A clear website, local search visibility and one well-run acquisition channel are often more useful than trying everything at once.

Can you help a Nepal-based business reach international customers?

We can plan appropriate advertising, search and website work for international audiences, subject to market, account and business requirements.

Do you guarantee top rankings or sales?

No. Rankings and sales depend on many factors. We work toward clear goals and show what the data supports.
