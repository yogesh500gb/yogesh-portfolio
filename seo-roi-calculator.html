How to use this SEO calculator

Start with a realistic monthly organic traffic target. Add the share you think will enquire, the share of enquiries that become customers and the average sale value. Then compare the modelled revenue to the expected monthly SEO cost.

Revenue is not profit. Search performance is not certain. The simple return shown above does not subtract product costs, staffing, fees outside SEO or customer fulfilment costs. Use it to explore assumptions, not to promise returns.
